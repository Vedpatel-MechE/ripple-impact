The handoff, without the black box

Four checkpoints from surplus to school-ready.

You keep control of the initial declaration. RIPPLE makes the work, cost, custody, and acceptance requirements explicit before a mission can proceed.

  1. 01

    Declare the batch

    Share aggregate device type, condition, quantity, location, and timing. No serial numbers are collected here.

  2. 02

    Confirm authority

    A later review checks ownership, disposition approval, locks, and data-sanitization requirements.

  3. 03

    Approve the mission

    See the proposed recipient, repair scope, funding target, custody plan, and failure route before agreeing.

  4. 04

    Receive the evidence

    Follow the batch through intake, wiping, repair, QA, delivery, rejection, or responsible recycling.
