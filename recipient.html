Need first, hardware second

You define what a useful outcome means.

RIPPLE should protect schools and charities from becoming the final stop for unusable donations. Every mission starts with a requirement and ends with your acceptance.

  1. 01

    Define the program need

    Share an aggregate quantity, use case, minimum device category, location, and deadline.

  2. 02

    Review the proposed fit

    See the source batch, expected specifications, repair scope, timing, support, and open risks.

  3. 03

    Confirm before work begins

    Your organization independently accepts the plan; no other participant can agree on your behalf.

  4. 04

    Inspect and accept delivery

    Confirm quantity and function, report exceptions, and close the mission only after usable devices arrive.

Start with a real program

Describe one aggregate technology need.

Tell RIPPLE what your organization can distribute, maintain, and put into service. This is an expression of need—not a guarantee that a device batch or funding is available.

A strong request explains
  • Who the program serves in aggregate
  • What learners will do with the devices
  • The minimum category that will work
  • Who can receive and support the equipment
Protect your learners

Do not submit names, demographic lists, free-lunch status, IEP information, grades, photos, or any other student-level data.
