A fairer work order

Take the tranche you can stand behind.

A shop should never have to accept an opaque truckload to participate. RIPPLE exposes the batch, requirements, funding, timeline, and failure route before you commit.

  1. 01

    Publish capacity

    State supported device types, services, monthly throughput, location, and typical turnaround.

  2. 02

    Review a proposed tranche

    Inspect batch evidence, sampling assumptions, recipient requirements, custody plan, and funding.

  3. 03

    Confirm scope and price

    Quote the actual labor, parts, timeline, warranty, and exceptions before the mission opens for funding.

  4. 04

    Document the work

    Record intake, sanitization, repair, QA results, rejected units, handoff, and approved milestones.
