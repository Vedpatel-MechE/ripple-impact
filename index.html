A ONE-MINUTE TOUR

How to use RIPPLE

  1. Pick a mission type. Devices, food, tutoring, or your own idea.
  2. Choose what you bring. Fill in a short card for assets, skills, money, or a community need.
  3. Complete the chain. Click the missing cards to add the other pieces.
  4. Compare plans. See costs, paid work, reach, risks, and the assumptions behind each estimate.
  5. Export a brief or explicitly save to the local server. Neither action verifies people, money, or impact.
