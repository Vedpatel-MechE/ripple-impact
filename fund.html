Know what has to happen before you pledge.

A RIPPLE campaign should open only after an asset owner, recipient, and repair partner have defined the mission. Funding closes the activation gap; it does not replace due diligence.

  1. 01

    Choose a mission

    Compare recipient, device quantity, work scope, budget, evidence requirements, and unresolved risks.

  2. 02

    Fund any amount

    Several small gifts can complete one defined activation budget. There is no investment or repayment.

  3. 03

    Follow the work

    See the mission move through funding, custody, repair, QA, delivery, and recipient review.

  4. 04

    Receive an impact record

    The final record separates dollars allocated, paid work, devices attempted, devices accepted, and failures.

Transparency by state, not storytelling

A payment is not yet impact.

RIPPLE keeps the stages separate so a pledge cannot be presented as a delivered device.

  1. 01PledgedA donor expresses intent or completes payment through the charitable partner.
  2. 02AllocatedFunds are assigned to the approved mission budget.
  3. 03Work paidAn approved invoice or milestone is reconciled.
  4. 04QA passedDevices meet the mission’s documented acceptance test.
  5. 05AcceptedThe school or charity confirms the usable delivery.
