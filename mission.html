Device passport

Every handoff leaves a record.

Custody milestones distinguish what a participant said from what another party accepted. Sample events below demonstrate the intended workflow.

  1. ✓

    Batch declared

    Participant attestation

    Forty laptops described by the sample asset contributor.

    Evidence expected: private asset manifest and disposition authorization
  2. 02

    Release and pickup

    Evidence pending

    Both releasing and receiving parties record quantity and visible condition.

    Evidence expected: signed handoff, timestamp, count, exception photos
  3. 03

    Sanitize and diagnose

    Not started

    The repairer records wipe result, condition grade, and parts required per device.

    Evidence expected: sanitization report and diagnostic checklist
  4. 04

    Repair and quality assurance

    Not started

    Devices must pass the agreed specification; failures follow the recycling route.

    Evidence expected: repair log, functional test, pass/fail count
  5. 05

    Recipient acceptance

    Not started

    The recipient—not RIPPLE—confirms quantity, specification, accessories, and exceptions.

    Evidence expected: delivery record and authorized acceptance

Claim-specific trust

No single “verified” badge.

Each claim has its own source, date, reviewer, and limitation. A real mission would never inherit trust from an unrelated check.
